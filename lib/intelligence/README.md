# Outlly Intelligence (lib/intelligence)

The Content Growth Engine behind the Content Intelligence page, the Growth Planner and Pip's `intelligence_overview`. Plan and history: `docs/INTELLIGENCE-AUDIT-R1231.md`.

## Module map

Pure (no I/O, no Prisma, safe in tests and on the client):

- `core.ts`: availability (unavailable, unsupported, zero), like-with-like format groups, topic index, signals, recommendation drafts, confidence.
- `reco-core.ts`: dedupe key, expiry, the number-preserving wording check.
- `planner-core.ts`: allowed actions and transitions, prefill.
- `outcome-core.ts`: an outcome against the same platform and format cohort.
- `brand-core.ts`: brand profile and agency client validation (scope keys, client account ids).
- `categories-core.ts`: category and topic validation.
- `prefill-core.ts`: a recommendation turned into a Content Studio draft (`/create?rec=`).
- `report-core.ts`: the Weekly Intelligence Report block (email and dashboard).
- `features-core.ts` (R1318): one published destination -> a versioned ContentFeatures row (counts and flags, never the copy) and PerfFeatures (derived totals; `measured` needs views and a day of age).
- `model/scorer-core.ts` (R1319): the `Scorer` interface and `baseline-ts-1` (cohort median shrunk toward a prior with n/(n+6), capped trait adjustments, cohort-spread range, no cohort row at or after asOf), plus `evaluate` / `accuracyWords`.
- `profile-core.ts` (R1318): ContentFeature rows -> CreatorProfileData (cadence, shares, medians, trait lifts) and the personalization ladder personal / blended / general / none.
- `entitlements-core.ts` (R1334): who gets the personal engine. One plan perk (`personal-intelligence`, Admin > Pricing) decides; `intelligenceAccessOf(entitlements)` derives the five capabilities (personal_intelligence / predictions / recommendations / clip_ranking / learning), the reason (perk, legacy-paid, free, plan-without-perk), the 402 message (`accessBlock`), the upgrade card copy and the cold-start line. Shared-model contribution is consent, not a capability.
- `consent-core.ts` (R1335): "Help improve Outlly AI", pure. `CONSENT_POLICY_VERSION`, the three data categories the shared pipeline reads (content_metadata, performance_signals, production_signals) with labels, `NEVER_INCLUDED`, the exact title and explanation the user agrees to, `consentState(rows)` (newest row wins; `current` false when granted under an older policy version; `deletion` from any row), `consentStatusLine`, `consentView` (the API shape).
- `shared-lineage-core.ts` (R1336): the shared pipeline's bookkeeping, pure. `validateShared(doc, contributorIds)` (no contributor id in the JSON, every bucket and statistic past the floors, builtAt a date), `compareShared(prev, next, prevSeq)` (common buckets, largest views-per-follower median move, anomaly when buckets or rows fall below half of the build before or a median moves > 50%), `buildLine`, `datasetVersion`, retention 90 days. Promotion is automatic unless validation fails: the consent promise (posts leave at the next rebuild) outranks a manual gate, so an odd build is served and flagged.
- `personal-reco-core.ts` (R1320): the creator's ModelStates -> personal recommendation drafts (a trait lift with both sides and samples, the format whose baseline beats the next by 25%), at most 2 per platform and 5 in all, only on platforms at level blended or personal; ids `personal:<rule>:<subject>:<platform>` so one live row per advice.

Server (touch Prisma, `import "server-only"`):

- `entitlements.ts` (R1334, server): `intelligenceAccess(userId)`, `requireIntelligence(userId, capability)` (throws the 402 `PLAN_LIMIT` feature `intelligence` the web client and the app turn into an upgrade prompt, and logs LearningEvent `entitlement_denied` once per owner, capability and UTC day), `hasIntelligence`, `entitledOwners`, `contributesShared` (R1335: the consent record, `consent.ts`). Gated: every `/api/intelligence/*` route except `sharing` and `feedback` (`insights` and `pulse` answer `{ entitled: false, upgrade }` softly), `intelligencePulse` (null), `personalRead`, `predictDraft`, the clipper's `hintsFor` (neutral hints), Pip's `predictions`, and `refreshOwner` / `sweepPersonal` through `refreshScope`: features for an owner with the perk OR consent (the shared model's lightweight signal), profiles / forecasts / model states / learning log only with the perk, nothing for neither (`skipped`). A downgrade stops at the next call and deletes nothing. Plan: `docs/intelligence-entitlements-plan.md`.
- `service.ts`: loadPostRecords (one post query and one groupBy, 60 s cache, at most 2000 destinations) and overview. R1249: `scopeKey` ("" or a client) picks the brand profile and the scope recommendations live under; `accountIds` narrows the numbers.
- `memory.ts`: Content Memory, brand profiles and clients, the 180 day backfill (`sweepBackfill`, a sweep step at minute % 10 === 7).
- `reco.ts`: stored recommendations, the read-only `storedFor`, AI wording (admin grant or the `ai-insights` plan perk, 30 a day).
- `planner.ts`: act() and the audit trail.
- `outcome.ts`: outcome links, measurement, the sweep step.
- `lineage.ts`, `categories.ts`: content identity and categories.
- `learning.ts` (R1318): `logLearning` with dedupeKey. `client-scope.ts` (R1319): the ?client= rule (owner only, stored profile, the client's target ids).
- `shared-core.ts` (R1324): shared learning, pure. `buildShared(rows)` turns many creators' measured rows into buckets (platform + format, and + category) holding n, distinct creators, the top creator's share and median/p25/p75 of views-per-follower, raw views and engagement rate; a bucket, and each statistic inside it over the rows that carry that number, is published only with >= SHARED_MIN_OWNERS (5) creators, >= SHARED_MIN_POSTS (20) posts and no creator above half, each creator capped at SHARED_OWNER_CAP (30) newest rows; a post joins only once its first week is over (COHORT_LAG_MS); no id survives into the document. `sharedFor(doc, {platform, format, categories, followers})` gives the scorer's `SharedBaseline` (category bucket first; views = median views-per-follower x followers, else raw median with a source line that says it is unscaled). `weeklyAccuracy` files evaluated PredictionRecords under the ISO week their outcome was known (MAPE / in-band / mean bias per week, non-overlapping) and `driftOf` gives the 4-vs-4-week verdict; `accuracyByWeek` (trailing 90-day rollups) is kept for the per-creator view only.
- `consent.ts` (R1335, server): the AiDataConsent record, append-only (one row per decision, newest = current, the table is its own audit). `grantConsent` / `withdrawConsent` / `requestDeletion` (idempotent; each writes the row and the `User.shareLearning` mirror in one transaction, the only writer of that column; LearningEvent `consent_granted` / `consent_withdrawn` / `consent_deletion_requested`), `currentConsent`, `hasSharedConsent`, `consentHistory`, `consentedOwnerIds` (mirror prefilters, table decides; stale mirrors counted), `pendingDeletions` / `closeDeletions` (the shared sweep rebuilds at once for an open request and marks it done), `consentSummary` (admin counts: granted, withdrawn, never asked, deletions, by policy version, by category, 30-day decisions). Never preselected: `shareLearning` defaults to false and `supabase/migrations/0003_r1335_consent_mirror_reset.sql` (run by `scripts/db-sync.mjs` after every push) resets any R1324 default-on mirror with no granted decision behind it. Route: GET/PATCH /api/intelligence/sharing (`{ action: grant | withdraw | request-deletion, source }`, owner only; open to every plan, reads no entitlement). UI: `components/intelligence/SharingRow.tsx` (title, explanation, what it covers, never-included list, status + policy version, withdraw, deletion request) under Your baselines, on the upgrade card and in Settings > Data & Privacy (`components/settings/AiConsentCard.tsx`).
- `shared-lineage.ts` (R1336, server): `recordBuild` (SharedDatasetBuild: seq +1, dataset version `sd-<seq>`, feature and document schema versions, consent policy version and categories, trigger, contributors, rows read and kept, rejections {unmeasured, noMetric, thinBuckets, truncated}, buckets, validation + notes, evaluation, anomaly, served, duration; an AdminEvent `intelligence_build` when flagged or failed), `syncContributions` (SharedContribution: one row per pseudonymous contributor, `contributorKey` = HMAC(AUTH_SECRET, owner id) so no user id is stored; consent id and policy version in force, categories, ContentFeature ids read (capped), first/last build, build count; everyone not in the build goes inactive), `purgeContributions` (a deletion request clears references, keeps the pseudonymous row as the record), `pruneBuilds` (90 days), `myContribution` (the creator's own counts), `buildsSummary` (admin counts).
- `jobs-core.ts` (R1337): the Intelligence job log, pure. Jobs `personal` (sweep step), `shared` (rebuild), `predict` (on-demand draft forecast); `summarizeRuns` (runs, failures, success rate, last run and error, p50 / p95 / max wall time per job), `sumPersonalRuns`, `predictLatency`, `percentile` (nearest rank), `errorText` (message only, 500 chars, no stack); retention 30 days, window 30 days, latency window 7 days.
- `management-core.ts` (R1337): pure rules behind Admin > AI Management. `accessDistribution({ users, candidates, freePlan })` runs `pickAccess` then `intelligenceAccessOf` over every live subscription and grant (free / perk / legacy-paid / plan-without-perk, by plan, by source, a Free plan that lists the perk is flagged); `summarizeDenials` (LearningEvent `entitlement_denied` by capability and reason), `retentionLine`, `freshnessOf`, `orphanedOwners`, `isolationVerdict`.
- `jobs.ts` (R1337, server): `recordJobRun` (never throws), `runJob(job, trigger, fn, { skip, summary })` (logs success with the result or failure with the error text and rethrows; `skip` leaves no row for "nothing to do"), `pruneJobRuns` (housekeeping slot), `jobsSummary`. Written by the sweep (`personal` every run, `shared` only when a rebuild ran), the admin rebuild (`shared`, trigger admin) and POST /api/intelligence/predictions (`predict`, trigger api: the measured forecast latency).
- `management.ts` (R1337, server): `intelligenceManagement()`, everything `/admin/intelligence/management` shows in one call: access distribution and denials (30 days), the consent summary with the current policy version and categories, the shared pipeline (builds summary, production document status, consented against creators with measured posts, compute time of 30 days of builds: time, not money, since builds run inside the sweep), the personal engine (workspace profiles by level and freshness, profiles retained after a downgrade, entitled accounts without a profile, forecasts, the job log), and the privacy ledger (consent decisions, deletion requests, retention lines against the oldest row on record, isolation checks run against the live rows: latest build validation, contributors against consent, mirror agreement, orphaned profiles, lineage by schema). A figure not measured yet is null and the page says so.
- `shared.ts` (R1324, server): `rebuildShared()` reads measured ContentFeature rows of creators whose newest consent decision is granted (R1335: `consentedOwnerIds`; no consent anywhere = an empty document, never everyone; 180 days, newest 50k) and stores the document as ModelVersion `shared-baselines-1` (kind `shared`); the row keeps whatever status the admin set (a retired document is refreshed but served to nobody); `loadShared()` (5 min cache, null unless status active), `followersOn` (newest snapshot per connected account), `sharedPriorFor`, `intelligenceGovernance()` (aggregates for the admin page: weekly accuracy from evaluated forecasts, versions newest first), `sweepShared` (every tick; rebuilds once the stored document is 55 min old, or at once when a deletion request is open; R1336: then purges the requesters' lineage references, closes the requests and prunes old builds). ## Invariants

- Deterministic numbers: every sum, median, lift and verdict is computed in a `*-core.ts` file. The same posts give the same answer.
- AI only rewords. The model never decides a number, a platform or a format; `validateWording` rejects any rewording that changes one. AI wording runs only with an AI grant covering `intelligence_wording` or a plan with the `ai-insights` perk (never Free by default), at most 5 per call and 30 per owner per UTC day, and falls back to the rule text.
- Plan-gated (R1334): the personal layer runs only for an owner whose plan carries `personal-intelligence`; the check is server-side on every protected operation, never a client flag. The workspace engine (R1231 to R1249) and the consent switch stay on every plan.
- Measured, not assumed (R1337): every number on Admin > AI Management is counted from rows when the page loads (IntelligenceJobRun for job rates and latency, LearningEvent for denials, the consent and build tables for the rest); nothing is hardcoded, and an unmeasured figure reads "not measured yet".
- Lineage (R1336): every served document traces to a SharedDatasetBuild row and every contributor to one pseudonymous SharedContribution row; nothing in either names a creator, and a deletion request clears the references. The served document holds bucket medians only; the data behind it is pseudonymised, not anonymous (the HMAC key exists), and the copy says so.
- Consent-first (R1335): nothing of a creator's enters the shared pipeline without a granted AiDataConsent row as their newest decision. Consent is never preselected, never implied by connecting a platform, independent of the plan (it unlocks nothing personal), withdrawable at any time, and a deletion request is honoured at the next sweep tick. `User.shareLearning` is a mirror written only by `consent.ts`.
- Tenant isolation: every read and write is filtered by `ownerId` (and `scopeKey` for agency clients). Another owner's ids are answered as not found.
- Idempotent jobs: regeneration returns the existing row per rule, outcomes are written at most once per 24 hours, expiry runs again safely, and the sweep step is bounded and never fatal.
- Honest gaps: a metric a platform does not give is unavailable, never 0; claims need at least 3 posts and always carry a confidence; outcomes are early signals, never causes.
- Predictions (R1319) are estimates from the creator's own measured posts: no measured post and no prior means null, never a number; every forecast names its model, level, sample and range, and is judged against the actual a week later. A model that cannot be evaluated yet says so.
