# Outlly Intelligence (lib/intelligence)

The Content Growth Engine behind the Content Intelligence page, the Growth Planner and Pip's `intelligence_overview`. Plan and history: `docs/INTELLIGENCE-AUDIT-R1231.md`.

## Module map

Pure (no I/O, no Prisma, safe in tests and on the client):

- `core.ts`: availability (unavailable, unsupported, zero), like-with-like format groups, topic index, signals, recommendation drafts, confidence.
- `reco-core.ts`: dedupe key, expiry, the number-preserving wording check.
- `planner-core.ts`: allowed actions and transitions, prefill.
- `outcome-core.ts`: an outcome against the same platform and format cohort.
- `brand-core.ts`: brand profile and agency client validation (scope keys, client account ids).
- `categories-core.ts`: category and topic validation.
- `prefill-core.ts`: a recommendation turned into a Content Studio draft (`/create?rec=`).
- `report-core.ts`: the Weekly Intelligence Report block (email and dashboard).
- `features-core.ts` (R1318): one published destination -> a versioned ContentFeatures row (counts and flags, never the copy) and PerfFeatures (derived totals; `measured` needs views and a day of age).
- `model/scorer-core.ts` (R1319): the `Scorer` interface and `baseline-ts-1` (cohort median shrunk toward a prior with n/(n+6), capped trait adjustments, cohort-spread range, no cohort row at or after asOf), plus `evaluate` / `accuracyWords`.
- `profile-core.ts` (R1318): ContentFeature rows -> CreatorProfileData (cadence, shares, medians, trait lifts) and the personalization ladder personal / blended / general / none.
- `entitlements-core.ts` (R1334): who gets the personal engine. One plan perk (`personal-intelligence`, Admin > Pricing) decides; `intelligenceAccessOf(entitlements)` derives the five capabilities (personal_intelligence / predictions / recommendations / clip_ranking / learning), the reason (perk, legacy-paid, free, plan-without-perk), the 402 message (`accessBlock`), the upgrade card copy and the cold-start line. Shared-model contribution is consent, not a capability.
- `personal-reco-core.ts` (R1320): the creator's ModelStates -> personal recommendation drafts (a trait lift with both sides and samples, the format whose baseline beats the next by 25%), at most 2 per platform and 5 in all, only on platforms at level blended or personal; ids `personal:<rule>:<subject>:<platform>` so one live row per advice.

Server (touch Prisma, `import "server-only"`):

- `entitlements.ts` (R1334, server): `intelligenceAccess(userId)`, `requireIntelligence(userId, capability)` (throws the 402 `PLAN_LIMIT` feature `intelligence` the web client and the app turn into an upgrade prompt, and logs LearningEvent `entitlement_denied` once per owner, capability and UTC day), `hasIntelligence`, `entitledOwners`, `contributesShared` (consent; Phase 2 moves it onto the consent table). Gated: every `/api/intelligence/*` route except `sharing` and `feedback` (`insights` and `pulse` answer `{ entitled: false, upgrade }` softly), `intelligencePulse` (null), `personalRead`, `predictDraft`, the clipper's `hintsFor` (neutral hints), Pip's `predictions`, and `refreshOwner` / `sweepPersonal` through `refreshScope`: features for an owner with the perk OR consent (the shared model's lightweight signal), profiles / forecasts / model states / learning log only with the perk, nothing for neither (`skipped`). A downgrade stops at the next call and deletes nothing. Plan: `docs/intelligence-entitlements-plan.md`.
- `service.ts`: loadPostRecords (one post query and one groupBy, 60 s cache, at most 2000 destinations) and overview. R1249: `scopeKey` ("" or a client) picks the brand profile and the scope recommendations live under; `accountIds` narrows the numbers.
- `memory.ts`: Content Memory, brand profiles and clients, the 180 day backfill (`sweepBackfill`, a sweep step at minute % 10 === 7).
- `reco.ts`: stored recommendations, the read-only `storedFor`, AI wording (admin grant or the `ai-insights` plan perk, 30 a day).
- `planner.ts`: act() and the audit trail.
- `outcome.ts`: outcome links, measurement, the sweep step.
- `lineage.ts`, `categories.ts`: content identity and categories.
- `learning.ts` (R1318): `logLearning` with dedupeKey. `client-scope.ts` (R1319): the ?client= rule (owner only, stored profile, the client's target ids).
- `shared-core.ts` (R1324): shared learning, pure. `buildShared(rows)` turns many creators' measured rows into buckets (platform + format, and + category) holding n, distinct creators, the top creator's share and median/p25/p75 of views-per-follower, raw views and engagement rate; a bucket, and each statistic inside it over the rows that carry that number, is published only with >= SHARED_MIN_OWNERS (5) creators, >= SHARED_MIN_POSTS (20) posts and no creator above half, each creator capped at SHARED_OWNER_CAP (30) newest rows; a post joins only once its first week is over (COHORT_LAG_MS); no id survives into the document. `sharedFor(doc, {platform, format, categories, followers})` gives the scorer's `SharedBaseline` (category bucket first; views = median views-per-follower x followers, else raw median with a source line that says it is unscaled). `weeklyAccuracy` files evaluated PredictionRecords under the ISO week their outcome was known (MAPE / in-band / mean bias per week, non-overlapping) and `driftOf` gives the 4-vs-4-week verdict; `accuracyByWeek` (trailing 90-day rollups) is kept for the per-creator view only.
- `shared.ts` (R1324, server): `rebuildShared()` reads measured ContentFeature rows of creators with `User.shareLearning` true (180 days, newest 50k) and stores the document as ModelVersion `shared-baselines-1` (kind `shared`); the row keeps whatever status the admin set (a retired document is refreshed but served to nobody); `loadShared()` (5 min cache, null unless status active), `followersOn` (newest snapshot per connected account), `sharedPriorFor`, `intelligenceGovernance()` (aggregates for the admin page: weekly accuracy from evaluated forecasts, versions newest first), `sweepShared` (every tick; rebuilds once the stored document is 55 min old). Routes: GET/PATCH /api/intelligence/sharing (owner only PATCH); admin GET/POST /api/admin/intelligence (`rebuild`, `status`; the active scorer cannot be retired) behind area `intelligence` (super). UI: the switch under Your baselines (`PersonalBaselines.tsx`), `/admin/intelligence`.
- `model/predict.ts` (R1319): `syncPredictions` (forecast every destination from the history before it, actuals after 7 days, weekly IntelligenceEvaluation per platform, CreatorModelState calibration), `predictDraft` (POST /api/intelligence/predictions, stored only for the owner's own post; R1324: takes the shared prior via `sharedLookupFor` when the creator's measured posts on that platform are below PERSONAL_MIN, as do model states, which record `shared: true` and fall back to a recomputation when the document is retired; stored only for a draft or scheduled post and never over a judged forecast; `syncPredictions` never takes the prior, so evaluations carry no hindsight), `predictionsOverview` (GET; `components/intelligence/PersonalBaselines.tsx`). GET /api/intelligence/platforms/:platform joins profile + baselines.
- `personal-reco.ts` (R1320): `personalRead` stores the personal drafts through `reco.ts` (same ids, statuses, planner actions and expiry as the workspace engine's) under the scope they belong to; `readOnly` matches stored rows and writes nothing. Routes: GET /api/intelligence/recommendations (stores), /insights (status + profiles + baselines + personal, read-only), /history (LearningEvents, or one recommendation's trail with ?recommendation=), POST /feedback (the planner's act). `planner.act` also logs a LearningEvent `recommendation_<event>` per real action. UI: `components/intelligence/PersonalRecommendations.tsx` ("Made for you").
- `features.ts`, `profile.ts` (R1318): ContentFeature refresh (young posts daily, all on a FEATURE_VERSION bump), CreatorProfile rebuild per scope and platform with one CreatorProfileSnapshot a week, the LearningEvent log (`logLearning`, dedupeKey), ModelVersion baseline row, and `sweepPersonal` (sweep slot minute % 10 === 2, stalest owners first). Routes: GET /api/intelligence/profile, /status, POST /refresh. Plan: `docs/intelligence-clipper-plan.md`.

Elsewhere:

- `pulse-core.ts` (R1325, pure; R1326 review: `sampleWords` names every sample a number leans on, blended ones included; `ClipperPulse.queued`; failed jobs count 30 days, accuracy words 90 days): the app-facing digest. `IntelligencePulse` = per platform {level, measured, top baseline {predicted, low, high, n, shared}, accuracyWords}, the first open recommendation, clipper queue counts, computedAt; `pickTop` (most measured format), `baselineLine` / `clipperLine` / `pulseLines` (one sentence each, every number with its sample, "no measured post yet" instead of a zero), `baselineItems` for the weekly report. `pulse.ts` (server): `intelligencePulse(ownerId, {scopeKey, targetIds})` reads `modelStates` once (handed to `personalRead(readOnly, states)`), the latest IntelligenceEvaluation per platform, and `clipperPulse` (ClipJob / GeneratedClip counts; a clip whose post went out counts as published before the row flips); `targetIds: []` gives the honest empty pulse, never the workspace's. Route: GET /api/intelligence/pulse (R1326, `{ pulse, lines }`, level `label` added, `?client=` via `resolveClient`), the one read the Android app uses for its Home card.
- `lib/pip/tools-intelligence.ts`: Pip's read-only tools: `intelligence_overview` (workspace scope, overview in readOnly mode), `predictions` (R1325: the owner's own baselines from the pulse, flagged estimate, link to /intelligence#intel-baselines) and `clipper_status` (R1325: queue counts, link to /clipper). `lib/pip/plan.ts` routes "baselines", "how many views will my next reel get", "clipper", "clips ready to post" to them before the analytics `forecast` (`STRONG_NOUN`; a how-to or capability question about those nouns still goes to the help bot, and a reach/engagement/likes question keeps its own tool). A scoped teammate reads through `targetIdsForAccounts` (client-scope.ts), the same subset a client scope gets.
- `components/home/IntelligencePulse.tsx` (R1325): the Home card for owners (not actors), fed by `intelligencePulse` behind the same soft timeout as the other Home cards; `lib/reports/weekly.ts` `withBaselines` adds the "Your baselines" section to the weekly email when the pulse is available.

## Invariants

- Deterministic numbers: every sum, median, lift and verdict is computed in a `*-core.ts` file. The same posts give the same answer.
- AI only rewords. The model never decides a number, a platform or a format; `validateWording` rejects any rewording that changes one. AI wording runs only with an AI grant covering `intelligence_wording` or a plan with the `ai-insights` perk (never Free by default), at most 5 per call and 30 per owner per UTC day, and falls back to the rule text.
- Plan-gated (R1334): the personal layer runs only for an owner whose plan carries `personal-intelligence`; the check is server-side on every protected operation, never a client flag. The workspace engine (R1231 to R1249) and the consent switch stay on every plan.
- Tenant isolation: every read and write is filtered by `ownerId` (and `scopeKey` for agency clients). Another owner's ids are answered as not found.
- Idempotent jobs: regeneration returns the existing row per rule, outcomes are written at most once per 24 hours, expiry runs again safely, and the sweep step is bounded and never fatal.
- Honest gaps: a metric a platform does not give is unavailable, never 0; claims need at least 3 posts and always carry a confidence; outcomes are early signals, never causes.
- Predictions (R1319) are estimates from the creator's own measured posts: no measured post and no prior means null, never a number; every forecast names its model, level, sample and range, and is judged against the actual a week later. A model that cannot be evaluated yet says so.
